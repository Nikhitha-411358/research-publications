### 🔹 Paper 1 — [AI-Driven Renewable-Aware Load Balancing in Hybrid Edge-Cloud Environment]

Conference: Springer 
Year:2026  
Authors: T. Nikhitha, S. S. Sameer & Beena B. M.

Abstract: 
With the rapid increasing of edge and cloud computing, efficient load balancing has become critical for ensuring optimal resource utilization and energy efficiency. This paper presents an AI-driven renewable-aware load balancing framework for hybrid edge-cloud environments. The proposed approach leverages Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithms to dynamically distributed computational workloads while prioritizing renewable energy utilization. Simulation results demonstrate that the framework improves system performance by reducing latency, enhancing throughput, and increasing renewable energy consumption compared to conventional load balancing strategies. This work highlights the potential of combining AI optimization techniques with renewable-aware policies to achieve sustainable, high-performance computing in hybrid edge-cloud architectures.

### 🔹 Paper 2 — Online Shopping Purchase Intention Prediction Using Machine Learning

Conference: I3ctcon 
Year:2025  
Authors: T. Nikhitha, S. S. Sameer & Debanjali Bhattacharya.

Abstract: 
The recent exponential growth of e-Commerce has made accurately predicting online shoppers' purchase intentions that is crucial for enhancing marketing strategies and resource al location. This study employs a comprehensive machine learning framework that analyzes session-level behaviors and user characteristics to identify potential buyers. The main objective of this paper is to predict whether a visitor to an e-commerce website will complete a purchase or not, based on their browsing behavior, demographics, and session attributes. Six classification models are evaluated, such as logistic regression, support vector machine (SVM), random forest, KNearest Neighbors (KNN), XGBoost, and Bagging. Data preprocessing includes handling class imbalance with SMOTE and selecting relevant features using the ANOVA F-test. Among different ML models, XGBoost achieved the highest accuracy of 89.2%. The interpretability of the model is further explained through LIME. Experimental re-sults demonstrate that ensemble methods outperform standalone classifiers in predicting purchase intent, providing a robust and explainable approach for real-world ecommerce applications.

### 🔹 Paper 3 — Search Listing Prioritization: Ordering Search Results Based on User Ratings

Conference: CSITSS
Year:2025  
Authors: Ananya Subramanian Raimee Madhuri Shukla Tirupathi Nikhitha Rugmini Krishna K. V. Vineetha S. Ullas

Abstract: 
This paper discusses creating a system that ranks search result using user-provided ratings and comments to improve the quality of search experiences. In this application, users can rate websites on a scale of 1 to 5 and leave comments that will be shown alongside each search result. The cumulative ratings determine the ranking of each website, with higher-rated websites appearing at the top. Only the top three comments are displayed, and they are randomized to ensure a diverse range of perspectives. Users can like or dislike a comment. Additionally, there is a dual feedback system that allows users to express their satisfaction or dissatisfaction with a website. This comprehensive feedback mechanism helps users make more informed choices and improve overall satisfaction by providing a better understanding of website quality.
